# The five levels for an existing deck

The person chooses how much may change. They are harness-slides' own intake levels, so follow its intake guidance for the one chosen. Whatever the level, the original is never changed and a new copy is delivered.

| Level | What may change | Steps after Classify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brand-only` | Nothing but the look: the brand's colours, fonts, logo and master layouts | Build, Deliver |
| `polish` | The brand, plus tidier layouts, spacing and overflow fixes | Build, Deliver |
| `redesign` | Each slide is designed afresh with the brand's layouts, icons and diagrams | Look, Build, Deliver |
| `rework` | The story: slides may be tightened, split, merged, dropped or reordered, then redesigned | Outline, Look, Build, Deliver |
| `selected` | Only the slides the person names, and only what they say | Build, Deliver |

## What each level keeps

- **brand-only.** Every slide, in its order, with the same points in the same order. No slide is added, dropped, merged or split.
- **polish.** Every word, and the order of slides and points, exactly. A better arrangement may be chosen where the original's layout hid what the slide says.
- **A slide with no words of its own** (a picture or a diagram alone), at brand-only and polish. The outline gives it a short title so it can be named. When the original's picture can be carried (a PNG or JPEG) and the brand's template has a layout that shows no title and has a picture slot, the build sets the slide on that layout with the picture in the slot, and draws nothing else on it: it gains no words. Otherwise (a diagram drawn in shapes, a picture of another kind, or no such layout) the slide is drawn as before, without words of its own but under its approved title, so nothing on it is lost, and Build notes on that slide: "This slide is drawn with a title because its picture can't be carried onto a layout without one."
- **redesign.** Every slide, its message and its qualifications. The words of every title, point, caveat and note are copied exactly. No new claim.
- **rework.** The deck's evidence. The text used is still copied from the deck; where the new story needs a connecting line the deck does not have, say so and leave it out rather than invent it. The proposed new shape is the outline, and the person approves it before anything is drawn.
- **selected.** Ask at intake which slides and what to change, and record it in the form as `selected`. Change only those, as a scoped patch through harness-slides. Every other slide must come out exactly as it went in, which harness-slides' compare confirms.

## Steps without a stop

Every level but `selected` first pulls the deck's outline out of its inventory (the `extract` step), and every level exports the brand contract before Build. Neither stops for the person: for `brand-only` and `polish` the next stop is Build. `selected` plans its patch instead of extracting.

## At Build

Show each changed slide beside the original. For `selected`, show only the slides that changed. Changes asked for after delivery are made the same way and delivered as the next numbered revision.
