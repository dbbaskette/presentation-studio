## The freedom: rework

The person wants the story reworked, so you are told in the task how. The text you use is still copied exactly from the inventory, but you may choose which slides to keep, in what order, and which to merge or split, where the task asks for it. Do not write a word of your own: if the new story needs a connecting line that the deck does not have, say so in your notes and leave it out. Notes you carry over stay with the slide whose points they belong to.
