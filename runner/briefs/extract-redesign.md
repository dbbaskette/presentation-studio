## The freedom: redesign

The person wants each slide designed afresh. Keep each slide's message and its qualifications: what its title claims, what its points say and its caveat. Design freely around them: choose the relation, the focal and the rhythm that serve the message, and group or order the points differently where that serves it. The words of every title, point, caveat and note are still copied exactly from the inventory. Keep every slide. Do not add a claim the deck did not make.
